Welcome to FotoFusion

Import, organize, and verify your photos in a few clicks. Here's a quick look at how it works.

Scan any source

Point FotoFusion at a memory card or folder. It finds your JPEGs and over 30 RAW formats, complete with thumbnails and metadata.

Organize by metadata

Build folder structures from capture date, camera, or lens — nine date formats to choose from. Save your setup as a preset for next time.

Preview before you copy

See the exact destination structure before anything moves. Include or exclude folders, and spot files that already exist.

Copy with confidence

Every file is copied and verified — Quick Verify or full MD5 — with a markdown report and collection statistics when it's done.
